# Clinical profile and outcomes of TCGA breast cancer patients

*A fictional report written for testing. It describes TCGA-BRCA release A.*

The median age at diagnosis was 58 years, and patients ranged from 26 to 90. Just over a quarter of patients, 27%, were diagnosed before age 50. Patients diagnosed at 65 or older made up roughly 35% of the cohort. More than two-thirds of patients with a recorded menopausal status were postmenopausal. Lobular carcinoma patients were diagnosed about six years later on average than patients with ductal carcinoma. Family history of breast cancer was most common among the youngest patients.

Stage II was the most frequent stage group, covering 58% of staged tumors. Stage III or IV disease was present in about a third of staged tumors.

Over follow-up, 202 deaths were recorded among the primary tumors. The overall death rate was 13.8% among primary tumors with survival data. The median overall-survival time on record was 843 days, a little over two years. Three in four stage IV patients died during follow-up, compared with fewer than one in ten stage I patients. HER2-enriched tumors had a lower death rate than Luminal A tumors. Postoperative radiation therapy cut the risk of death by roughly a third.
