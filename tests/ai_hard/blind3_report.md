# Somatic mutations and copy-number changes in TCGA breast tumors

*A fictional report written for testing. It describes TCGA-BRCA release A.*

Somatic alterations in this release follow the familiar breast cancer pattern. PIK3CA is the most frequently mutated gene, altered in 34.5% of sequenced primary tumors. TP53 follows at 33.4%, and it is mutated more than twice as often as the third-ranked gene, TTN. CDH1 mutations appear in 102 tumors, about 13% of those sequenced. GATA3 is mutated in 12.9% of sequenced tumors.

TP53 mutations are concentrated in basal-like tumors, where 85% carry one, against roughly one in ten Luminal A tumors. PIK3CA mutations are about seven times more common in Luminal A than in basal-like tumors. Among lobular carcinomas, 59% carry a CDH1 mutation, compared with under 2% of ductal carcinomas. MAP3K1 mutations predicted a better response to endocrine therapy in Luminal A disease.

On the copy-number side, high-level MYC amplification is present in 21.2% of tumors with GISTIC calls. CCND1 is amplified in between 17% and 20% of tumors. PTEN is deeply deleted in 56% of tumors. Just over half of the tumors scored HER2-positive by immunohistochemistry carry an ERBB2 amplification. It is these TP53 mutations that drive the aggressive clinical course of basal-like disease.
