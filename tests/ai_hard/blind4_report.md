# Receptor status and protein levels in TCGA breast cancer

*A fictional report written for testing. It describes TCGA-BRCA release A.*

Estrogen receptor status is positive in 77% of tumors with a known result, and progesterone receptor status in 67%. HER2 is positive by immunohistochemistry in 164 primary tumors, close to a quarter of those with a definitive result. Triple-negative disease accounts for 116 tumors, about 16% of those with all three receptors known. Tumors that are ER-negative but PR-positive are uncommon, numbering 61 in total. ER positivity is nearly universal among lobular carcinomas, at 96%.

The ER protein comparison includes 698 primary tumors with both RPPA data and a definitive ER result. Mean ER-alpha protein is 2.18 in ER-positive tumors against -0.87 in ER-negative tumors, a gap of about three units. HER2 protein is on average lower in HER2-enriched tumors than in Luminal B tumors. GATA3 protein in ER-positive tumors exceeds that in ER-negative tumors by more than two units. Where both the RNA-seq call and the 2012 label exist, the two agree for 90% of tumors.

Patients with the highest HER2 protein levels benefited most from trastuzumab. Smoking history showed no association with ER status. Most tumors in the release were diagnosed in 2010 or later. Protein data therefore adds a useful check on clinical receptor calls.
