# Key findings: TCGA-BRCA cohort review

*A fictional research report written for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

## Key findings

- Data completeness: 535 of the 1,247 primary tumors have all five data layers (survival, mutation, copy number, RNA and protein).
- Every one of the 742 primary tumors with protein data also has RNA-seq data.
- Mortality: 202 deaths among the 1,236 patients with survival data (16.3%).
- Mean overall-survival follow-up is 843 days.
- HER2-enriched tumors have the highest crude death rate of any RNA subtype (20.9%).
- Normal-like is a sizeable subtype: 119 tumors, 12.4% of RNA subtype calls.
- Basal-like tumors are almost all ER-negative: 124 of 136 with a known ER status (91.2%).
- Mean cyclin B1 protein (RPPA CYCLINB1) is lower in Basal-like than in Luminal A tumors.
- CDH1 is mutated in 34.5% of sequenced primary tumors.
- Deep deletions of PTEN are seen in 56 tumors (5.2% of tumors with copy-number data).
- Infiltrating ductal carcinoma is the dominant histology, at roughly 71% of primary tumors.
- 204 primary tumors are infiltrating lobular carcinoma.
- The majority of patients are postmenopausal.
- Diagnosis years span 1988–2013, and 2010 is the busiest single year with 225 diagnoses.
- Lobular tumors respond poorly to neoadjuvant chemotherapy compared with ductal tumors.
- PIK3CA-mutant tumors (273 of 791 sequenced, 34.5%) gain the most from endocrine therapy, so PIK3CA status should guide treatment choice.
- About 38% of tumors are Nottingham grade 3.
