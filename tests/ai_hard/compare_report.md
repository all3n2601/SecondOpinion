# Hard report: comparisons, rankings and group statistics

*Our own test report. Every sentence is about TCGA-BRCA release A. Expected verdicts are in compare_report.expected.json.*

Basal-like patients were diagnosed younger than Luminal A patients. Basal-like patients were the youngest at diagnosis of any RNA subtype. Luminal A patients were the oldest at diagnosis of any RNA subtype. Luminal B patients share the cohort's median age at diagnosis of 58 years.

TP53 is mutated more often than PIK3CA. PIK3CA is the most frequently mutated gene in the release. TTN ranks third among mutated genes. TP53 mutations are most common in Basal-like tumours among the RNA subtypes. PIK3CA mutations are rarest in Basal-like tumours.

HER2-positive tumours by immunohistochemistry are fewer than ERBB2-amplified tumours. Infiltrating lobular carcinoma is the second most common histology. Stage II is the most common stage. Postmenopausal women outnumber premenopausal women about three to one.

Mean ERALPHA protein is higher in ER-positive than in ER-negative tumours by about 3 units. The median recorded survival time is about 2.3 years.
