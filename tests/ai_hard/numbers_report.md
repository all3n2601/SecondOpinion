# Hard report: numbers written every way

*Our own test report. Every sentence is about TCGA-BRCA release A. Expected verdicts are in numbers_report.expected.json.*

Nearly eleven hundred primary tumours are in the release. The release holds one thousand two hundred and forty-seven samples in all. A hundred and thirty-nine samples are normal tissue. Seven samples are metastases. Twelve men are among the primary tumours.

Roughly a third of primary tumours with a mutation call carry a TP53 mutation. PIK3CA is mutated in about a third of tumours with mutation data. Fewer than one in ten tumours with mutation data carry a PTEN mutation. In 2.8% of tumours with mutation data BRCA1 is mutated. CDH1 mutations occur in 12.9 per cent of tumours with mutation data. GATA3 is mutated in ninety-seven tumours. Nine in every hundred tumours with mutation data have a KMT2C mutation.

TP53 mutations are roughly eight times as common in Basal-like as in Luminal A tumours. Basal-like tumours outnumber HER2-enriched tumours by more than two to one. Luminal A tumours are three times as common as Basal-like ones. Half of ER-negative tumours with an RNA call are Basal-like.

ERBB2 is amplified in one tumour in eight with copy number data. Twice as many tumours carry a MYC amplification as an ERBB2 amplification. Deep PTEN deletions appear in five dozen tumours.
