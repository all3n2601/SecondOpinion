# Who is in the data: samples, layers and slides in TCGA-BRCA

*A short fictional research summary prepared for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

TCGA's breast cancer collection links clinical records, molecular profiles and pathology images through shared barcodes. The release contains 1,247 samples from 1,101 patients. Besides the 1,101 primary tumors, there are 139 normal tissue samples and 9 metastases.

Molecular coverage is uneven across layers. Mutation calls are available for 791 primary tumors, far fewer than the 1,080 with copy-number profiles. RNA sequencing covers 1,097 primary tumors, leaving only four without expression data. Protein (RPPA) data exist for 742 primary tumors, about 70% of the cohort.

Combining layers shrinks the cohort quickly. Of the primary tumors with mutation calls, 785 also have copy-number data. Only 535 primary tumors carry all five molecular and outcome layers, just under half of the primary tumors.

Pathology images round out the picture. The slide manifest lists 3,111 pathology slides, of which 1,133 are frozen sections. At least one diagnostic slide is available for 1,061 primary tumors. Slide quality was uniformly high, with fewer than 2% of slides failing pathology review.

We recommend that every analysis state up front which data layers its cohort requires.
