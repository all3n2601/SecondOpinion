# Intrinsic subtypes and receptor status in TCGA breast tumors

*A short fictional research summary prepared for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

PAM50 assigns each tumor to one of five intrinsic subtypes based on gene expression. Of the 1,101 primary tumors, 844 received a PAM50 call from RNA sequencing. Luminal A is the most common subtype, at roughly half of subtyped tumors. Luminal B ranks second, followed by HER2-enriched and then basal-like tumors.

The balance between groups is striking. Luminal A tumors outnumber basal-like tumors by about three to one. The normal-like group is the smallest, with 23 tumors.

Clinical receptor testing tells a consistent story. Clinically, 77% of primary tumors with a recorded ER result were ER-positive. Progesterone receptor positivity was lower, at 64%. HER2 was positive by immunohistochemistry in 164 tumors, about 29% of those with a positive or negative result. In all, 116 tumors were negative for ER, PR and HER2.

Subtype also matters for treatment research. Basal-like tumors were the most likely to respond to platinum chemotherapy.

We recommend reporting subtype and receptor status side by side in future summaries.
