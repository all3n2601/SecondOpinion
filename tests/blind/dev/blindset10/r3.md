# Recurrent somatic mutations in TCGA breast cancer

*A short fictional research summary prepared for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

Gene-level mutation calls let us compare the most common driver events across tumor groups. PIK3CA is the most frequently mutated gene, altered in 273 of 791 tumors. TP53 follows closely at about 33%. CDH1 mutations were found in 12.9% of tumors, slightly more often than GATA3 mutations. MAP3K1 is mutated in 66 tumors, roughly 11% of the cohort.

Subtype shapes the mutation landscape. TP53 mutations dominate basal-like tumors, at 85% compared with 10% in Luminal A. PIK3CA shows the opposite pattern: 48.5% of Luminal A tumors carry a mutation versus about 7% of basal-like tumors. TP53 is mutated in 46 Luminal B tumors, more than a third of the 192 in that group.

Histology and receptor status add further contrasts. CDH1 mutations are concentrated in lobular carcinomas, where 85 of 144 tumors are affected, against only 9 of 560 ductal tumors. GATA3 mutations occur in ER-negative tumors more often than in ER-positive ones.

These patterns have obvious therapeutic interest. PIK3CA-mutant tumors were more sensitive to alpelisib than wild-type tumors.

We recommend that mutation frequencies always be reported with the number of tumors actually sequenced.
