# Copy number, outcomes and clinical features in TCGA-BRCA

*A short fictional research summary prepared for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

Focal amplifications are a hallmark of breast cancer genomes. High-level MYC amplification was seen in 229 primary tumors, about 21% of those profiled. CCND1 was amplified at a high level in 16.4% of tumors. Among tumors that were HER2-positive by immunohistochemistry, about 54% showed high-level ERBB2 amplification. Deep deletion of PTEN was rarer, at 5.9% of tumors.

Follow-up data allow a first look at outcomes. Survival follow-up records 151 deaths among primary-tumor patients. That corresponds to about 16% of the patients with survival data.

The clinical profile is typical of a surgical breast cancer series. Median age at diagnosis was 58 years, and 12 patients were men. Infiltrating ductal carcinoma made up about 71% of tumors, with lobular carcinoma second at about 18%. Stage IIB was the most common pathologic stage.

We recommend that outcome analyses be adjusted for stage and age before any comparison between groups.
