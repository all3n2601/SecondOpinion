# Abstract: Protein-level correlates of receptor status and histology in TCGA-BRCA

Protein abundance offers a measurement layer between gene expression and clinical phenotype. Using release A of TCGA-BRCA, we examined reverse-phase protein array data for 742 primary tumors. ER-positive tumors (n = 529) had a mean ER-alpha level of 2.18, against -0.87 in ER-negative tumors (n = 169). Progesterone receptor protein was likewise higher in PR-positive than PR-negative tumors, with a mean difference of 0.32. HER2 protein averaged 1.41 in HER2-positive tumors and 0.61 in HER2-negative tumors. GATA3 protein was lower in Luminal A than in basal-like tumors. E-cadherin protein was markedly reduced in lobular carcinomas (mean -1.89 across 101 tumors) relative to ductal carcinomas. These protein differences translated into better responses to endocrine therapy among tumors with high ER-alpha. Protein layers deserve a place in future multi-omic summaries of this cohort.
