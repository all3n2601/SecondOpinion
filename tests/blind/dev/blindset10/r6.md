# Figure legends and table notes: TCGA-BRCA release A overview

**Figure 1. Data-layer coverage of primary tumors.** (A) Bars show the number of primary tumors in each layer: survival 1,097, RNA 1,097, copy number 1,080, mutation 791 and protein 742. (B) RNA and protein overlap in 742 primary tumors, so every primary tumor with protein data also has RNA. Bar colors distinguish molecular from clinical layers.

**Figure 2. PAM50 composition.** (A) Subtype shares among 844 subtyped primary tumors: Luminal A 49.9%, Luminal B 22.7%, basal-like 16.7%, HER2-enriched 7.9%, normal-like 2.7%. (B) Luminal A and Luminal B together account for 75% of subtyped tumors. Colors follow the conventional PAM50 palette.

**Figure 3. Pathology slides.** Frozen sections outnumber diagnostic slides, 1,978 to 1,133. Images were annotated for tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes by two pathologists. Thumbnails are shown at reduced resolution.

**Table 1 notes.** ER-negative patients were younger, with a median age of 55 years versus 59.5 years in ER-positive patients. Basal-like tumors had the highest death rate of any subtype, 19 of 140. Percentages in the table are rounded to one decimal place.

**Table 2 notes.** High-level MYC amplification was found in 46.7% of basal-like tumors with copy-number data, compared with 9.6% of Luminal A tumors. Amplification calls use GISTIC thresholded values.
