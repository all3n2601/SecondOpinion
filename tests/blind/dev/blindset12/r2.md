# Lab meeting transcript: "What is mutated in TCGA-BRCA?"

*Transcript of a Thursday lab-meeting talk, lightly edited. Slides refer to the TCGA-BRCA release A files.*

**Slide 1: The usual suspects, and one surprise**

Speaker: Okay, let us start with the big picture. TTN is the third most frequently mutated gene, behind PIK3CA and TP53. TTN shows up in 137 tumors, so roughly one in four. It is a huge gene, so take that with a grain of salt.

**Slide 2: The long tail**

Speaker: MUC16 and KMT2C are essentially tied, 78 versus 77 mutated tumors. BRCA1 is mutated in 22 tumors and BRCA2 in 25. The median tumor carries 35 mutated genes.

**Slide 3: Co-occurrence**

Speaker: Only 57 tumors carry both TP53 and PIK3CA mutations. Patients whose tumors carried both mutations relapsed earlier because the two drivers cooperate.

**Slide 4: Splitting by receptor status and histology**

Speaker: TP53 is mutated in more than three quarters of ER-negative tumors, 138 of 178, against about 20% of ER-positive tumors. FOXA1 mutations are three times more common in ductal than in lobular tumors. PIK3CA is mutated in 46.5% of lobular tumors and 38.3% of ductal tumors.
