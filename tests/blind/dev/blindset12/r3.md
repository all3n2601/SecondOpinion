# Response to reviewers

Dear Editor,

We thank both reviewers for their careful reading of our manuscript describing the TCGA-BRCA release A cohort. Our point-by-point responses follow.

## Reviewer 1

**Comment 1.1.** *The authors should describe the male patients.*

Response: We agree and have added a paragraph. All 12 male patients in the cohort had ER-positive tumors. Their ages ranged from 44 to 84 years.

**Comment 1.2.** *Please clarify how many patients had distant disease.*

Response: We now report that 22 tumors were recorded as M1 at diagnosis, while 24 carried a Stage IV label. Death was recorded in 15 of the 20 Stage IV patients.

**Comment 1.3.** *Tumor size should be summarized.*

Response: More than half of the tumors were classified as T2. We have added the full T-category table to the supplement.

## Reviewer 2

**Comment 2.1.** *Is age at diagnosis similar across histologies?*

Response: Median age was 61 years for lobular carcinomas and 58 years for ductal carcinomas. Mucinous carcinomas were diagnosed at the oldest median age, 70 years.

**Comment 2.2.** *Did ER-negative patients die more often?*

Response: They did, 41 of 238 (17.2%) compared with 12.4% of ER-positive patients. Disease-specific deaths numbered 93 across all primary tumors.

**Comment 2.3.** *What about endocrine treatment?*

Response: Hormone therapy adherence exceeded 90% among ER-positive patients.

We hope the revised manuscript is now suitable for publication.

Sincerely,
The authors
