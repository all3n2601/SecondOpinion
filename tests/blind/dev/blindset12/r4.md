# Methods and Results: Focal copy-number events in TCGA-BRCA

## Methods

We analysed the TCGA-BRCA release A files. Primary tumors were identified by the sample-type code in the barcode. Gene-level GISTIC2 calls were used, with a value of 2 treated as high-level amplification and -2 as deep deletion. Subtypes were taken from the RNA-seq PAM50 calls and receptor status from the clinical immunohistochemistry fields. Protein levels came from the reverse-phase protein array file.

## Results

High-level FGFR1 amplification was present in 135 tumors (12.5%). ZNF703 was amplified slightly more often, in 145 tumors. Co-amplification of CCND1 and FGFR1 occurred in 37 tumors, about 3% of those profiled. AKT3 and MCL1 amplifications were each seen in about 14% of tumors. Overall, 870 primary tumors had at least one high-level amplification somewhere in the genome.

CCND1 amplification was largely confined to ER-positive disease: 158 of 796 ER-positive tumors versus 12 of 233 ER-negative tumors. RB1 deep deletion was most frequent in HER2-enriched tumors, at 5 of 67. CDKN2A was deeply deleted in 4.4% of tumors and amplified in 1.9%.

At the protein level, mean HER2 protein was 2.1 in the 96 tumors with high-level ERBB2 amplification versus 0.2 in the 634 without. PTEN-deleted tumors had higher PTEN protein than tumors without the deletion.

These results support integrating copy-number and protein layers in subsequent analyses.
