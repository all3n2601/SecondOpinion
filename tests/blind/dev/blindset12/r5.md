# Interim Progress Report to the Foundation, Aim 3: The protein layer

**Reporting period:** months 13 to 18. **Dataset:** TCGA-BRCA, release A.

## Progress against Aim 3

Aim 3 asks whether reverse-phase protein measurements add information beyond RNA subtypes. During this period we completed the first round of group comparisons.

AR protein was higher in Luminal A than basal-like tumors (mean 1.38 vs -0.06). P53 protein showed the reverse, higher in basal-like tumors. Among tumors with both mutation and protein data, TP53-mutant tumors (n = 190) had higher P53 protein than wild-type tumors (n = 348). EGFR protein was higher in ER-positive than ER-negative tumors. HER2-enriched tumors had a mean HER2 protein of 2.02, more than eight times the Luminal A mean. P-cadherin protein was higher in ER-negative tumors. Phospho-ER-alpha (S118) was higher in Luminal A tumors, at 0.39 versus -0.13 in basal-like tumors.

## Preliminary modelling

Adding protein data improved relapse prediction beyond PAM50 in our pilot models.

## Next steps

The next reporting period will focus on validating these comparisons. We will submit the next report at month 24.
