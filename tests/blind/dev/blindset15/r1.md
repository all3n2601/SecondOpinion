# Practice brief: Hormone receptors and intrinsic subtypes in TCGA-BRCA

*A short fictional research summary written for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

Clinical receptor testing and expression-based subtyping are two lenses on the same tumors. Comparing them shows where the two systems agree and where they part ways.

Nearly all Luminal A tumors with a recorded ER result were ER-positive, 393 of 402. Among basal-like tumors, 12 were nonetheless recorded as ER-positive. HER2-enriched tumors were split, with 21 ER-positive and 40 ER-negative cases.

The two hormone receptors usually travel together. Most ER-positive tumors were also PR-positive, 682 in all. Only 16 tumors were ER-negative but PR-positive. About 30% of primary tumors with a PR result were PR-negative.

HER2 testing is the least tidy of the three. HER2 immunohistochemistry was equivocal in 197 tumors. Of the 164 HER2-positive tumors, the largest share came from the HER2-enriched subtype. Triple-negative tumors were mostly basal-like: 77 of the 116 carried that label.

Receptor status also shapes treatment. ER-positive patients who received tamoxifen lived longer than those who did not.

We suggest that future summaries report both receptor and subtype labels for every tumor.
