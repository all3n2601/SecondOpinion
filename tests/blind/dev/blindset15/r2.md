# Results

## 3.2 Somatic alterations in primary tumors

We first summarized gene-level mutation calls and then turned to thresholded copy-number calls.

PIK3CA and TP53 were the two most commonly mutated genes, together affecting 480 of 791 tumors. SYNE1 was mutated in 50 tumors, slightly more than PTEN. CBFB was mutated in 18 tumors, the same number as SF3B1.

Mutation frequencies differed by receptor status. KMT2C mutations were nearly twice as frequent in ER-positive as in ER-negative tumors (11.1% versus 6.2%). PTEN mutation frequency was almost identical in ER-positive and ER-negative tumors, at about 6.5% and 6.2%. HER2-positive tumors had a higher TP53 mutation rate than HER2-negative tumors, 43.8% versus 34.4%.

At the copy-number level, MDM2 was amplified in 63 tumors. IGF1R amplification occurred in 51 tumors, about 5%. ESR1 itself was rarely amplified, in 3.5% of tumors. EGFR amplification (23 tumors) was twice as common as KRAS amplification.
