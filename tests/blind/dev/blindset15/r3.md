# Abstract

**Stage, subtype and crude mortality in TCGA breast cancer**

**Background.** Public cohorts are often used to explore outcome differences between clinical groups. **Methods.** We linked clinical annotations of TCGA-BRCA (release A) to the overall survival endpoint and tabulated crude death rates. **Results.** Of 1,097 primary-tumor patients with survival data, 151 died during follow-up. Crude mortality was 3.5% in Stage IA and 75% in Stage IV. Patients with Stage IIIA disease died at a rate of 16.7%, compared with 9.5% for Stage IIA. Lobular carcinomas had a lower crude death rate than ductal carcinomas (11.3% versus 13.5%). HER2-enriched tumors had a death rate of 20.9%, nearly double that of Luminal B tumors (16.7%). Luminal A patients had the lowest death rate among the five subtypes. Disease-specific survival events were recorded for 83 patients, about 10% of those with disease-specific data. These differences reflect the greater efficacy of endocrine therapy in luminal disease. **Conclusions.** Crude rates should be interpreted with caution until adjusted analyses are available.
