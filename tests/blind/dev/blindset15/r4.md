# Table 2. Copy-number and protein highlights in TCGA-BRCA primary tumors (release A)

| # | Finding | Data layer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MCL1 was amplified at a high level in 147 primary tumors. | Copy number |
| 2 | PIK3CA was amplified at a high level in 54 tumors, 5.0% of the copy-number cohort. | Copy number |
| 3 | TP53 deep deletion was seen in 51 tumors. | Copy number |
| 4 | Basal-like tumors had the most PTEN deep deletions of any subtype, 23 of 135. | Copy number, RNA subtype |
| 5 | Luminal A and Luminal B tumors contributed the same number of FGFR1 amplifications, 41 each. | Copy number, RNA subtype |
| 6 | Luminal A tumors had higher HER3 protein than basal-like tumors (mean 0.36 versus 0.11). | Protein, RNA subtype |
| 7 | ER-positive tumors had higher AR protein than ER-negative tumors, with means of 1.33 and 0.38. | Protein, clinical |
| 8 | Phospho-HER2 (Y1248) averaged 1.26 in HER2-positive tumors and 0.27 in HER2-negative tumors. | Protein, clinical |
| 9 | PTEN protein was lower in Luminal A than in basal-like tumors. | Protein, RNA subtype |

**Notes.** Copy-number findings use gene-level GISTIC2 calls, where 2 marks high-level amplification and -2 deep deletion. Protein values are RPPA levels as distributed. Receptor status comes from the clinical immunohistochemistry fields. Amplification calls were confirmed by FISH in a random 10% subset of tumors.
