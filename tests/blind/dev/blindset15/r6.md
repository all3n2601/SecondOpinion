# Practice brief: Layer overlaps for integrative analysis

*A short fictional research summary written for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

Integrative studies live or die by how many tumors appear in every layer they need. Here we tabulate the overlaps that matter most.

Of the 791 primary tumors with mutation calls, 789 also have RNA data. Mutation, copy number and RNA together are available for 783 primary tumors. Mutation and protein data overlap in 538 primary tumors. Protein and copy number overlap in 742 primary tumors. Just two primary tumors lack every molecular layer.

Non-tumor samples behave differently. Metastatic samples contribute RNA for seven and protein for five samples, but no mutation calls. Normal tissue samples account for 399 of the slides. Frozen top sections are the most common slide kind, with 1,814 slides.

Sample age may also matter. Samples collected before 2005 have lower RNA quality.

We recommend publishing the exact sample list behind every integrative result.
