# Copy number changes and follow-up in TCGA-BRCA

*A short fictional research summary for practice, describing the TCGA-BRCA files in release A.*

Copy-number changes are a defining feature of many breast tumors. We used the GISTIC thresholded calls, treating a value of 2 as high-level amplification and -2 as deep deletion.

Thresholded copy-number calls cover 1,080 primary tumors. High-level MYC amplification was present in 229 tumors, about 21% of those with copy-number calls. ERBB2 was amplified in 133 tumors, a little over 12%. CCND1 amplification was less common than ERBB2 amplification. Deep deletion of PTEN was found in 56 tumors, slightly more than the 45 with deep deletion of CDKN2A.

Among tumors scored HER2-positive by immunohistochemistry and present in the copy-number file, just over half (87 of 162) showed ERBB2 amplification, versus 9 of 556 HER2-negative tumors.

Turning to follow-up, the survival file records 151 deaths among the 1,097 primary tumors it covers. That works out to an overall death rate of roughly 16%. Among Luminal A, Luminal B, basal-like and HER2-enriched tumors, the HER2-enriched group had the highest share of deaths, 14 of 67. ERBB2-amplified tumors therefore carry a worse prognosis and should be prioritised for HER2-targeted treatment.

Longer follow-up would make these survival comparisons more informative.
