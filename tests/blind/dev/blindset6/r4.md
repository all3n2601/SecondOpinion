# Who is in the cohort: demographics, stage, histology and slides

*Fictional summary written for audit practice. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files of release A.*

Before any molecular analysis, it helps to know who the patients are. This brief draws on the clinical table and the slide manifest.

Twelve of the primary tumors come from male patients, and all twelve were ER-positive. The median age at diagnosis was 58 years, with patients ranging from 26 to 90. Fewer than 6% of patients were diagnosed before age 40. Basal-like tumors were diagnosed at a younger median age (54) than Luminal A tumors (61). Black patients were more likely than white patients to have basal-like tumors.

Stage II disease dominated, accounting for 622 tumors, or roughly 58% of those assigned stage I to IV. Stage IV tumors were rare, with 24 cases. Infiltrating ductal carcinoma made up about 71% of primary tumors, and infiltrating lobular carcinoma accounted for 203 tumors.

The slide manifest lists 3,111 slides, of which 1,133 are diagnostic FFPE sections. Frozen top sections are the single most common slide kind, with 1,418 slides, and every primary tumor has at least one slide.
