# Protein layer results

## Results

Of 1,101 primary tumors, 742 have RPPA protein data and 535 have all five layers (survival, mutation, copy number, RNA and protein). All 742 tumors with protein data also have RNA data. Mean ER-alpha protein was 2.18 in ER-positive tumors (n = 529) and -0.87 in ER-negative tumors (n = 169), a gap of about 3.1 units. HER2 protein was likewise higher in HER2-positive than HER2-negative tumors (mean 1.41 vs 0.16). GATA3 protein averaged 2.36 in ER-positive tumors against 0.74 in ER-negative tumors. Mean p53 protein was higher in ER-positive than in ER-negative tumors. Among PAM50 groups, mean ER-alpha was higher in Luminal A than in Luminal B tumors. ER-negative tumors were also more often histological grade 3 than ER-positive tumors. These comparisons are descriptive and were not adjusted for any covariates.
