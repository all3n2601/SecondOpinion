# Data inventory notes, release A

These notes were jotted down while preparing a TCGA-BRCA analysis plan; they are fictional and meant for practice.

- Normal tissue: 139 samples, each from a patient who also has a primary tumor sample in the release.
- Metastatic samples: 8 in total.
- RNA-seq: 1,097 primary tumors and 114 normal-tissue samples.
- Primary tumors missing mutation data: 301.
- TP53 mutation frequency: 24% of primary tumors.
- PTEN deep deletion: about 5% of tumors with copy-number calls.
- Treatment: patients who received trastuzumab had fewer deaths than those who did not.

Open questions will be tracked separately.
