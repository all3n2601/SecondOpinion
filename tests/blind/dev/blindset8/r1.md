# Research brief: What is in the TCGA breast cancer release

*A short fictional research summary written by the organizers for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files in this repository (release A from `fetch.py`).*

Before any analysis, it helps to know exactly which samples a release holds. The release contains 1,247 samples from 1,101 patients. Of these, 1,101 are primary tumors, 139 are normal tissue samples and 9 are metastases.

Two details about who is in the cohort are worth noting. Twelve primary tumors come from male patients, a little over 1% of all primary tumors.

Pathology slides are plentiful. Diagnostic slides number 1,133, against 1,978 frozen sections. Frozen sections therefore make up about 68% of all slides, and only three primary tumors have no slide at all. All but one of the 139 normal tissue samples have at least one slide. Pathologists reading these slides agreed on tumor grade in nine of ten cases.

We recommend that every analysis report the sample types it includes.
