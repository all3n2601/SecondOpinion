# Research brief: RNA subtypes in TCGA breast cancer

*A short fictional research summary written by the organizers for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files in this repository (release A from `fetch.py`).*

PAM50 subtypes remain the standard way to group breast tumors by gene expression. Of the 1,101 primary tumors, 844 have an RNA subtype call. Luminal A is the most common subtype at 421 tumors, about 50% of subtyped primaries.

Luminal B is second with 192 tumors, followed by HER2-enriched. Basal-like tumors account for about 19% of subtyped primaries. There are roughly three Luminal A tumors for every Basal-like tumor.

Normal-like is the rarest call among primary tumors, with 23 tumors. Across all sample types, 119 samples are called Normal-like, most of them normal tissue samples.

Subtype may also matter clinically. Basal-like tumors were more likely than Luminal A tumors to respond to neoadjuvant chemotherapy.

We recommend that subtype shares always be reported with their denominator.
