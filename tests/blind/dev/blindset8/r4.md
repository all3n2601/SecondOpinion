# Research brief: Hormone receptor and HER2 status in TCGA breast cancer

*A short fictional research summary written by the organizers for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files in this repository (release A from `fetch.py`).*

Receptor status drives how breast cancers are classified in the clinic. Among primary tumors with a recorded ER result, 808 were ER-positive and 238 ER-negative. That puts ER positivity at about 77%. PR positivity is lower, at about 61% of tumors with a known PR result.

HER2 was positive by immunohistochemistry in 164 tumors, which is 22.5% of all 1,101 primary tumors. A further 179 tumors had an equivocal HER2 result.

Triple-negative tumors number 116, about 16% of the 722 tumors with a positive or negative call for all three receptors. Most triple-negative tumors are Basal-like, 77 of the 116. ER-positive patients who received tamoxifen had fewer recurrences than those who did not.

We recommend that receptor results be reported together with the number of tumors scored.
