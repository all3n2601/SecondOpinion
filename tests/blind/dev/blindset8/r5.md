# Research brief: Somatic mutations in TCGA breast cancer

*A short fictional research summary written by the organizers for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files in this repository (release A from `fetch.py`).*

Mutation data is where many breast cancer studies begin. Of the 791 primary tumors with mutation calls, PIK3CA is mutated in 273 and TP53 in 264. PIK3CA is therefore the most frequently mutated gene, at about 35%. CDH1 ranks third among all genes, mutated in about 13% of tumors.

TP53 mutations are far more common in Basal-like tumors (about 85%) than in Luminal A tumors (about 10%). None of the 106 Basal-like tumors with mutation calls carries a GATA3 mutation. CDH1 is mutated in 85 of 144 lobular carcinomas, about 59%, against under 2% of ductal carcinomas.

Genes linked to hereditary risk also appear in the file. BRCA1 is mutated in 2.8% of tumors and BRCA2 in 3.5%. Tumors with PIK3CA mutations responded better to alpelisib than tumors without them.

We recommend that mutation rates be compared within subtypes rather than across the whole cohort.
