# Research brief: Copy number changes and deaths in TCGA breast cancer

*A short fictional research summary written by the organizers for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files in this repository (release A from `fetch.py`).*

Copy number changes are a hallmark of breast cancer genomes. Of the 1,080 primary tumors with copy number data, 229 carry a high-level MYC amplification, about 21%. ERBB2 is highly amplified in 133 tumors, or 12.3%. CCND1 amplification is more frequent than MYC amplification.

Deep deletions are rarer: PTEN is deeply deleted in 56 tumors and CDKN2A in 74. Among HER2-positive tumors by immunohistochemistry, about 54% show high-level ERBB2 amplification.

Survival follow-up adds outcome information. Overall, 151 of the 1,097 primary tumors with survival data belong to patients who died, about 14%. Among the 20 patients with Stage IV tumors, 15 died. Patients with ERBB2-amplified tumors who were treated with trastuzumab lived longer than those who were not.

We recommend that copy number findings be linked to outcomes only with careful adjustment for stage.
