# Research brief: Patient characteristics and protein levels in TCGA breast cancer

*A short fictional research summary written by the organizers for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files in this repository (release A from `fetch.py`).*

Clinical descriptors help put molecular results in context. The median age at diagnosis among primary tumors is 58 years, with patients ranging from 26 to 90. Patients under 40 make up about 11% of primaries.

Infiltrating ductal carcinoma accounts for 784 primaries and infiltrating lobular carcinoma for 203. Stage IIA is the most common stage, with 358 tumors. Stage IV disease was recorded for 20 primaries, fewer than 2%.

Protein data tells a consistent story. Luminal A tumors have the highest mean ER-alpha protein of any RNA subtype. E-cadherin protein is lower in lobular than in ductal carcinomas. Lobular carcinomas were more often detected by screening mammography than ductal carcinomas.

We recommend that protein comparisons always name the groups and the number of tumors in each.
