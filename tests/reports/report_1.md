# Test report 1: Subtype mix in TCGA breast cancer

*A fictional summary written by the SecondOpinion team to test the auditor. Several statements are wrong on purpose.*

This summary looks at how PAM50 subtypes are spread across the TCGA-BRCA release.

The release has 1,150 primary tumors.

LumA tumors account for nearly 50% of primary tumors with an RNA subtype call. Basal tumors make up between 10 and 30 percent of subtyped primary tumors.

Luminal A is 49.9% of subtyped primary tumors, and these patients respond well to hormone therapy.

The median age at diagnosis was 58 years.

Subtype labels should be read with care.
