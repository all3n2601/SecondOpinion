# Results

Primary tumours numbered 1,101 (normal tissue, 139; metastatic, 7). Median age at diagnosis was 58 years (range 26-90); mean age, 59.5 years. Patients over 60: 493 (44.9%). ER-positive disease was common (808/1,046; 77.3%); PR positivity was lower (699 of 1,043; 67.0%). Triple-negative tumours (ER-, PR-, HER2-) comprised 16.1% of the 722 tumours with all three receptors known (n = 116). HR+/HER2- tumours numbered 455. Stage II predominated (622 of 1,075 staged tumours; 58%); stage IV was rare (n = 20; 1.9%). Invasive lobular carcinoma accounted for 203 cases (19.4%). Male patients: 13 (1.2%). Node-positive disease was present in 561 tumours (52% of those with known nodal status). CDH1 mutations were concentrated in lobular tumours (85/144; 59.0%) versus ductal tumours (9/560; 1.6%). Deaths: 151 (13.8% of 1,097 with survival data). Fifteen of the 20 stage IV patients died. GATA3 mutations were absent in basal tumours (0/106) and present in 12.3% of all sequenced tumours.
