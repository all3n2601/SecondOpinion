# Key findings

- About half (50%) of subtyped primary tumours are Luminal A.
- HER2-enriched tumours are the least common PAM50 subtype.
- Two in five tumours carry a TP53 mutation.
- TP53 and PIK3CA mutations co-occurred in only 57 tumours.
- MYC is amplified (GISTIC +2) in roughly one fifth of tumours.
- Copy-number gain or amplification of CCND1 (GISTIC 1 or 2) affects over a third of tumours.
- Copy-number loss of TP53 (GISTIC -1 or -2) is seen in about 61% of tumours.
- Patients older than 65 make up about 29% of the cohort.
- Fewer than 70 patients were diagnosed under 40.
- Every slide in the archive is a diagnostic FFPE slide.
