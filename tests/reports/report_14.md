# Brief: The subtype landscape of TCGA breast tumors

*A short fictional summary for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files (release A).*

Breast cancer is better understood as a family of molecular diseases than as a single illness. The release holds 1,247 samples from 1,101 patients, including 139 normal tissue samples and 7 metastases. Of the primary tumors, 844 carry a PAM50 subtype call from RNA sequencing.

Luminal A makes up about 50% of the subtyped tumors, and Luminal B is the second most common subtype at roughly 23%. Basal-like tumors outnumber HER2-enriched tumors by about three to one. Normal-like tumors form the smallest group, with 23 samples.

Hormone receptor status tracks subtype closely. Among Basal-like tumors with a known ER result, 91% are ER-negative, while Luminal A tumors are almost always ER-positive (98%). Basal-like tumors respond better to chemotherapy than luminal tumors, so subtype should guide first-line treatment.

We plan to extend this overview to other cancer types.
