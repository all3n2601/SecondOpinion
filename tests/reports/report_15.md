# Brief: Receptor status and clinical features

*A short fictional summary for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files (release A).*

Receptor testing shapes how breast cancer is classified and treated in the clinic. Among primary tumors with a recorded estrogen receptor result, 77% are ER-positive. Progesterone receptor positivity is lower, at about 64%. HER2 was positive by immunohistochemistry in 164 of 728 tumors with a definitive result. Triple-negative tumors, negative for all three receptors, number 116, or about 16% of tumors with all three results known.

The median age at diagnosis was 58 years, and the youngest patient was 26. Twelve patients in the cohort are men. Stage II disease accounts for more than half of staged tumors, while stage IV is rare, with 20 cases. Infiltrating lobular carcinoma makes up about 22% of tumors with a recorded histology.

Patients with ER-positive tumors were less likely to die because hormone therapy prevents relapse. These descriptive figures give useful context for the molecular analyses that follow.
