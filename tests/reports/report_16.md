# Brief: Somatic mutations in TCGA breast tumors

*A short fictional summary for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files (release A).*

Somatic mutations help explain why breast tumors behave so differently. Gene-level mutation calls are available for 791 primary tumors. TP53 is the most frequently mutated gene in the cohort, altered in about 33% of tumors. PIK3CA mutations are found in 273 tumors.

Mutation patterns differ sharply by subtype. TP53 mutations are far more common in Basal-like tumors (85%) than in Luminal A tumors (10%). CDH1 mutations are concentrated in lobular carcinomas, where about 59% of tumors carry one, compared with under 2% of ductal carcinomas. GATA3 is mutated in roughly 14% of tumors, and no Basal-like tumor carries a GATA3 mutation. MAP3K1 mutations occur in 76 tumors.

Patients with PIK3CA-mutant tumors benefit most from PI3K inhibitors. Mutation data remain a cornerstone of tumor profiling.
