# Brief: Copy number and protein layers

*A short fictional summary for testing. It describes the TCGA-BRCA files (release A).*

Combining DNA, RNA and protein measurements gives a fuller picture of each tumor. Copy number profiles are available for 1,080 primary tumors and protein profiles for 724. Every primary tumor with protein data also has RNA data.

ERBB2 is amplified in about 12% of primary tumors with copy number data. MYC amplification is less common than ERBB2 amplification. Among tumors scored HER2-positive by immunohistochemistry, 87 show ERBB2 amplification.

At the protein level, ER-alpha is markedly higher in ER-positive than in ER-negative tumors, with a mean difference of about 3.1 units. E-cadherin protein is higher in lobular than in ductal carcinomas. High Ki-67 staining was most common in Basal-like tumors.

Integrating these layers should be a priority for future work.
