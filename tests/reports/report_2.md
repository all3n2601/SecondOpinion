# Test report 2: How complete is the molecular data?

*A fictional summary written by the SecondOpinion team to test the auditor. Several statements are wrong on purpose.*

- 535 of 1,101 primary tumors have all five data layers.

- Every primary tumor has RNA-seq data.

The release also includes 139 normal tissue samples. In addition, 12 samples are from male patients.

These gaps matter when choosing an analysis.
