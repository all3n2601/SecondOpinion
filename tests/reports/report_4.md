# Test report 4: Mutations, receptors and survival

*A fictional summary written by the SecondOpinion team to test the auditor. Several statements are wrong on purpose.*

The release holds 1,247 samples from 1,101 patients.
There are 7 metastatic samples and 139 normal-tissue samples.
TP53 is mutated in about a third of primary tumors with mutation data.
PIK3CA is the most frequently mutated gene.
GATA3 mutations occur in more than 10% of tumors.
Basal-like tumors outnumber HER2-enriched tumors by about two to one.
Roughly 15 percent of primary tumors are HER2-positive.
Between 70% and 80% of primary tumors are ER-positive.
ER-positive patients live longer than ER-negative patients.
More than 150 deaths are recorded among primary tumor patients.
Protein data covers most triple-negative tumors.
The cohort is large enough for a Luminal A versus Basal-like comparison.
Stage II tumors make up about half of primary tumors with a recorded stage.
Ki-67 staining was high in most Luminal B tumors.
Tumors with BRCA1 mutations are best treated with PARP inhibitors.
