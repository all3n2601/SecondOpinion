# Mutations and copy number in TCGA breast tumors

*A short fictional research summary describing the TCGA-BRCA files (release A).*

Somatic mutations and copy-number changes are the genomic backbone of the TCGA project. Of the 791 primary tumors with gene-level mutation calls, 264 (33%) carried a TP53 mutation. PIK3CA was the single most frequently mutated gene, altered in roughly 35% of sequenced tumors. CDH1 mutations were found in 15% of tumors. GATA3 and MAP3K1 were each mutated in more than 10% of tumors.

TP53 mutations were far more common in Basal-like tumors (85%) than in Luminal A tumors (10%). Among infiltrating lobular carcinomas with mutation data, a majority (59%) carried a CDH1 mutation. BRCA1 and BRCA2 mutations were each seen in fewer than 3% of sequenced tumors.

On the copy-number side, high-level ERBB2 amplification (GISTIC value of 2) was present in 133 of 1,080 tumors with copy-number data. MYC was amplified at high level in about one in five tumors. CCND1 amplification was more frequent than ERBB2 amplification. Deep deletions of PTEN were seen in between 8% and 10% of tumors. Patients whose tumors carried PIK3CA mutations responded better to alpelisib. Larger, treatment-annotated cohorts will be needed to translate these findings.
