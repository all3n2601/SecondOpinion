# Who is in the TCGA-BRCA release

*A short fictional research summary describing the TCGA-BRCA files (release A).*

This summary describes who is in the TCGA-BRCA release and what data exist for them. The release contains 1,247 samples from 1,101 patients, including 139 normal tissue samples and seven metastases. Survival, mutation, copy number, RNA and protein data are all available for 553 primary tumors. Protein (RPPA) profiles exist for 742 primary tumors, fewer than the 791 with mutation calls.

The median age at diagnosis was 58 years, and patients ranged from 26 to 90 years old. Twelve patients were men. Infiltrating ductal carcinoma accounted for about 72% of tumors, and infiltrating lobular carcinoma for about 25%. Stage II disease was the most common stage at diagnosis (622 tumors), while only 20 tumors were Stage IV.

In total, 151 patients died during follow-up, between 13% and 15% of those with survival data. Disease-specific deaths numbered 83. Diagnostic (FFPE) slides outnumber frozen sections in the slide manifest. Over 1,000 patients have at least one diagnostic slide. Women with a higher body-mass index had more aggressive tumors. Together these layers make TCGA-BRCA a strong teaching resource.
